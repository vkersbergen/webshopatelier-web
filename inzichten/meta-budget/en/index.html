How much should you budget for Meta ads?

By WebshopAtelier · Updated 21 November 2026

Start with how much you can spend to win one new order. Then choose a test budget your store can afford and agree in advance when to stop. A standard daily spend is of little use until you know your margin and your target.

In this article
  • How much can you afford to pay for a purchase?
  • Turn your target CPA into a capped test
  • When should you pause or increase spending?

How much can you afford to pay for a purchase?

Start with order revenue after discounts and excluding VAT. Deduct product costs, any shipping you pay, packaging, transaction fees and a realistic allowance for returns. What remains is your contribution before advertising and fixed costs. Leave room for running the business and for your target profit.

Worked example. With €60 in order revenue and €35 in variable costs, €25 remains. Your break-even advertising cost for this first purchase is €25 before fixed costs. If you want €7 left for fixed costs and profit, your target cost per acquisition (CPA) is €18. Only factor in profit from repeat purchases once your own customer records show how many people return and how long that takes.

Turn your target CPA into a capped test

Suppose you have €180 for media and a target CPA of €18. At that spend, you need ten attributed purchases to meet your target. That sets a decision threshold. It doesn’t predict sales. With six purchases your actual CPA is €30, so an immediate budget increase would expose you to more risk. Record ad production and campaign management separately when calculating total cost.

A daily budget suits an ongoing campaign. A lifetime budget with an end date is a better fit for a fixed-cost test. The platform minimum can depend on your currency and campaign settings. Check what your account accepts for the setup you plan to use. Read Meta’s budget settings documentation.

When should you pause or increase spending?

  • Clicks but few visits to the intended products. Open the ad link yourself. Check whether the message matches the destination and whether the landing page loads promptly on mobile.
  • Checkout starts but few paid orders. Check the payment step and how orders are recorded before committing more budget. Test checkout on mobile.
  • Measured CPA is below target. Compare the sales Meta attributes to ads with paid orders in your store for the same period. Check returns and distinguish first-time from existing customers. Increase the budget once results hold across several orders and you have the cash available for the next test.

Account for costs outside Meta too. If you spend €180 on media and €60 on creative and gain ten new customers, your cost per new customer including production is €24. Media CPA is €18 if all ten purchases are attributed to the campaign. Ask for media, production and management on separate lines when pricing Meta and TikTok advertising. Compare the €24 with the contribution from a first order.
