How much does an AI chatbot for your website cost per month?

By WebshopAtelier · Updated 21 November 2026

Your monthly bill typically includes a platform fee, usage charges and, in some cases, user licences. Setup and maintenance add to the total. As a benchmark, Intercom lists Fin for an existing help desk from $0.99 per billable outcome. At that rate, 200 billable outcomes start at $198 in usage charges, before any other fees. Check Intercom’s current pricing and terms.

In this article
  • What does setup cover?
  • Compare quotes using the same unit
  • When does a chatbot pay for itself?

What does setup cover?

An assistant that explains your services and shares contact details needs few information sources. A store chatbot that answers order questions also needs a way to identify the right customer and order, suitable access permissions and rules for handing conversations to a person. Ask which integrations, languages and acceptance tests the quote covers.

Compare quotes using the same unit

Per message, per conversation and per resolution can produce very different bills. A question followed by four more messages may count as one conversation with one provider, while another charges for every message. Ask each provider to price the same volumes for a quiet month and your busiest month. Check what happens if a customer comes back with the same issue.

When does a chatbot pay for itself?

Add up licences, usage, time spent updating answers and the cost of staff handling transferred conversations each month. Spread the setup fee over the period you expect to use the bot. Compare that total with the staff time it demonstrably saves. Track how many customers still send an email after the bot counts their enquiry as resolved.

Suppose the bot costs €150 a month including maintenance and each enquiry it resolves on its own saves five minutes. At €30 per staff hour, each resolution saves €2.50. Under those assumptions you need sixty genuine resolutions to break even. Exclude enquiries that a colleague has to finish. Use your own volumes when requesting a quote for a custom AI assistant.
